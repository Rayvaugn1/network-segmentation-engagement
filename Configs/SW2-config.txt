# SW2 – Access Switch (Users & Finance Segment)

## Role
SW2 provides endpoint connectivity for VLAN 10 (Users) and VLAN 30 (Finance).

## Key Configuration Functions

- VLAN 10 assigned to user endpoints (PC1)
- VLAN 30 assigned to finance routing device (R2)
- Trunk uplink to SW1 carries all VLAN traffic
- PortFast enabled for endpoint interfaces

## Design Notes

- No inter-VLAN routing performed at switch layer
- VLAN separation enforced via access port assignments only
